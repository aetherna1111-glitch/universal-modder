# Report: Lumin's economy (slice 16), 2026-10-14

## Asked
A dialogue screen for Lumin in the style of Prominence II's NPC dialogues with the launcher's look; trade in his own
points for holy items (Paladins & Priests), lightstone dust and something no trader sells; points for killing the
bosses of the pack's main story (the FTB Quests `main_story` chapter: the Night Lich, the Withered King, …); after
five tasks with nothing bought, a special hardcore-but-possible task rewarded with his blade, which keeps every
attribute and changes only its owner id to the player's; an angelic-harp weapon he takes up afterwards.

## Done
- Bosses taken from the pack itself: `main_story.snbt` of Prominence II: Hasturian Era 4.1.1 (the version in the
  owner's `mods.csv`), read from the Modrinth archive by range requests (only the quest chapter was fetched). 13
  kill tasks in story order plus the Trial of the Envoy (five late bosses, never dying).
- Core (`dev.luminary.core.npc.trade`): `Quest`, `ShopOffer`, `PlayerLedger`, `TradeService`, `TradeRules`; ledgers in
  the NPC record, codec `trade` section (schema v2, no migration needed: the section is optional).
- Fabric: `TradeManager` (hooks on `AFTER_DEATH` for kills and player deaths, actions re-checked server side,
  snapshot packet `luminary:trade_open`, action packet `luminary:trade_action`), right-click opens the screen,
  sneak + right-click talks; the gear screen moved to a tab.
- Items: `luminary:lightstone_dust`, `luminary:envoy_seal` (unique, Blessing on use), `luminary:angelic_harp`
  (Chord of Light; Lumin's own, named, drawn in combat once the blade is TRANSFERRED, replaces the beam within
  6 blocks). 16×16 textures drawn in code.
- The blade's hand-over: `HolySwordItem.transferTo` replaces `LuminaryOwner` (the NPC id) with
  `LuminaryOwnerPlayer` (the player's UUID) and the name line with the player's name; everything else is untouched.
- Client: `TradeScreen` (navy and gold, portrait, points, tabs, progress bars, tooltips with the boss names and
  counts, his line from the character sheet at the bottom, Talk/Done).
- Data: 14 quest files, `shop` in `lumin.json`, 7 new line groups, ru/en lang. Docs: `docs/TRADE.md`, README.
